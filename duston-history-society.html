Duston History Society

We are a small History Society based in Duston. We meet on the second Monday of each month, except in July, August, and May, when we go on a coach trip. We meet at St. Crispin's Social Club on Berryfields Road in Duston. £5 entry on the door of £40 for the year. Talks are given on historical events in Northamptonshire and the wider area.
