Northamptonshire Natural History Society (NNHS)

County-wide · Specialist body

The Northamptonshire Natural History Society has been part of the local community since 1876. For 150 years, we have been recording and preserving Northamptonshire’s fascinating natural and local history.

Our collections include fossils, historic photographs, journals and records gathered over generations. The History Section meets at the Humfrey Rooms, Northampton, NN1 1LD, on the last Thursday of each month at 2.30pm. Everyone is welcome – you don’t need to be a member or an expert to come along.
