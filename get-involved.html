Link to Northants History Network

If you have a website for your group, organisation, project or local history work, we would be delighted if you would link to Northants History Network. The card below gives your visitors an easy way to find us.

Northants History Network ❦ Connecting Northamptonshire’s Past with Today’s Community northantshistorynetwork.org.uk

How to add the card to your website

  1. Copy the code below.
  2. Paste it into an HTML or custom-code block on your website.
  3. Save or publish the page. The card will link directly to the Northants History Network website.

If you are unsure how to add it to your particular website, please email Northants History Network and we will be happy to help.
