Our Story

The Northants History Network grew from a simple observation. While working at Discover, the local history department of Northamptonshire Central Library, our founder regularly met people researching similar subjects — authors, students, researchers and curious minds — who were often unaware of others sharing their interests. It seemed natural to ask: what if we connected them?

From the very first meetings, one thing became clear. Many people were already members of organisations with formal memberships, fees and AGMs, but what they also wanted was somewhere less structured — a relaxed and welcoming space to meet like-minded people and share their enthusiasm for local history.

And so the Northants History Network was born.

Established in 2022, the Network meets four times a year in a friendly, informal setting and welcomes anyone with an interest in the history of Northamptonshire — whether you are researching a book, studying, exploring your family or local history, or simply enjoy discovering more about the county’s past.

Support from Northampton Central Library

“Northampton Central Library is pleased to support the Northants History Network, which grew from people using the Library’s local history collections and wanting to connect with others who shared their interests. The Network offers a relaxed, welcoming space for anyone curious about Northamptonshire’s past to meet, share ideas and learn together. Supporting the Network reflects the Library’s role as a community hub, not just a place for books, but a place where people, stories and local history come together.”

James Toal
Northamptonshire Central Library Manager
