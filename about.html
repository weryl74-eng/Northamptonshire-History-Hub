About NHN

— ✦ —

Northants History Network is an independent community project established in 2022 by founder Cheryl Workman. It brings together people and organisations who care about Northamptonshire’s history and heritage.

Our aim is to make the county’s history easier to discover by connecting local history groups, researchers, authors, museums, archives, heritage organisations, event organisers and interested members of the public.

This website is being built as a community gateway. It brings together stories, events, organisations and useful resources, and its Northamptonshire timeline will continue to grow as knowledge and contributions are added.

Help the Network grow

If you have a local event, historical resource, group, story or specialist subject to share, visit our Get Involved page or email us.

Accessibility statement

— ✦ —

We want the Northants History Network website to be welcoming and usable for as many people as possible.

As the site develops, we aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. This is an aim, not a claim that every page currently meets that standard.

What we are doing

  • Using clear headings, readable text and meaningful link wording.
  • Providing text alternatives for informative images wherever possible.
  • Supporting keyboard navigation, browser zoom and visible keyboard focus.
  • Reviewing colour contrast and layouts at different screen sizes.
  • Considering accessible alternatives when new content is added.

Known limitations

The website has not yet had a full independent accessibility audit. Some older visual material and third-party content may not yet be as accessible as we would like. In particular, embedded Facebook posts are controlled by Meta and may not meet the same accessibility standard as the rest of the site.

Tell us about a problem

If you cannot access something, need information in a different format, or notice an accessibility problem, please email northantshistorynetwork@gmail.com. Please tell us which page you were using and what went wrong. We will consider a reasonable alternative or adjustment.

For more information about the standard we are working towards, see the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2.

Privacy notice

— ✦ —

This notice explains what personal information Northants History Network may receive, why we use it and the choices available to you.

Who is responsible for your information?

Northants History Network is responsible for the personal information described in this notice. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to northantshistorynetwork@gmail.com.

Information we may receive

If you email us or contribute to the Network, we may receive your name, email address, organisation or group, area, website or social-media link, the content you submit, an image and credit, and records of any permission you give us.

Get Involved form: our contribution form is provided by Tally. Information entered into the form is handled by Tally and made available to Northants History Network when it is submitted.

How and why we use it

We use personal information to reply to enquiries, assess and manage contributions, maintain contact with contributors, and operate and improve the Network. We normally do this because it is in our legitimate interests to run the community project. Where we ask for permission to publish personal details, words or images, we rely on and record that permission. We may also use information where necessary to meet a legal obligation.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled by services that help us operate the website and communicate, including GitHub Pages for website hosting, Google/Gmail for email, and Tally for the Get Involved form. These providers have their own privacy information:

  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Tally Privacy Policy

We may also share information if required by law or if you specifically ask us to publish or pass it to another organisation.

Facebook and links to other websites

The homepage contains embedded Facebook content, and the site links to Facebook and other external websites. Meta or another third party may receive technical information, use cookies or process information when its content loads or when you follow a link. Their use of information is governed by their own policies. See the Meta Privacy Policy.

Northants History Network does not currently use its own advertising or analytics cookies. This does not prevent third-party content from using cookies or similar technology.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose for which it was collected, or to meet a legal requirement. Contact details for an enquiry are removed when they are no longer needed. Records connected with published contributions or permissions may be retained while the content remains published and for as long as reasonably needed to demonstrate the permission given.

Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to ask for a copy of your personal information, correct it, request deletion or restriction, object to particular uses, or withdraw consent. To make a request, email us at the address above.

If you have a concern, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You can also make a complaint to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
