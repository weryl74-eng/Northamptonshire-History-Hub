Archives & heritage partners

— ✦ —

Places to begin when you want to consult original records, explore collections or discover county heritage.

Northamptonshire Archives Service

Explore more than 800 years of Northamptonshire’s history through written records, maps, drawings and photographs held at Wootton Hall Park.

Visit the archives service

Northampton Museum & Art Gallery

Discover local history, archaeology, art and the museum’s nationally significant footwear collection in the centre of Northampton.

Visit the museum

Chester House Estate

Explore a nationally significant heritage site in Irchester with evidence of more than 10,000 years of human activity.

Visit Chester House Estate

Northamptonshire Heritage Forum

Find museums, galleries, historic houses, heritage sites and societies across the county.

Visit the Heritage Forum

County-wide & specialist bodies

— ✦ —

Organisations with county-wide collections, specialist knowledge or a particular area of historical interest.

Northamptonshire Family History Society

Support for family historians researching Northamptonshire people and places, including publications, indexes and member resources.

More

Northamptonshire Record Society

Publishes historical records and research relating to the county, with a long tradition of making source material accessible to researchers.

More

Northamptonshire Industrial Archaeology Group

Promotes research and recording of the county’s industrial heritage, including buildings, sites, transport and working landscapes.

More

Northamptonshire Natural History Society (NNHS)

The Northamptonshire Natural History Society has been part of the local community since 1876, recording and preserving the county’s natural and local history. Its collections include fossils, historic photographs, journals and records gathered over generations.
