Northamptonshire Natural History Society

The Northamptonshire Natural History Society has been part of the local community since 1876. For 150 years, we have been recording and preserving Northamptonshire’s fascinating natural and local history, with collections including fossils, historic photographs, journals and records gathered over generations. Our History Section meets at the Humfrey Rooms, Northampton, NN1 1LD, on the last Thursday of each month at 2.30pm. Everyone is welcome – you don’t need to be a member or an expert to come along.

Great Addington History

Great Addington History explores the story of this Northamptonshire village, its people, buildings and Nene Valley landscape from the Iron Age to the modern day. The site includes material on archaeology, village buildings, maps, All Saints Church, transport and life through different historical periods.
