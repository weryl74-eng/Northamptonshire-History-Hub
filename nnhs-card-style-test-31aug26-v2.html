Northamptonshire Natural History Society (NNHS)

The Northamptonshire Natural History Society has been part of the local community since 1876. For 150 years, we have been recording and preserving Northamptonshire’s fascinating natural and local history. Our collections include fossils, historic photographs, journals and records gathered over generations.
