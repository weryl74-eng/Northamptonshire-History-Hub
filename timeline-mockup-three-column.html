Medieval Northamptonshire

Castles, churches, monasteries, markets and powerful local families reshaped Northamptonshire after the Norman Conquest. Towns developed around trade and religion, while many of the county's surviving medieval buildings still reveal how people lived, worshipped and worked.

Early-modern Northamptonshire

Religious upheaval, the English Civil Wars, estate building and changing patterns of local power transformed Northamptonshire's towns and countryside. The county's churches, houses, battle sites and archives preserve many traces of this turbulent period.
